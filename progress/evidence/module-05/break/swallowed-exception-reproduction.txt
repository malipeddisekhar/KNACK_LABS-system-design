4. PRODUCTION IMPACT ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Silent Data Loss: Clients are falsely informed that their link was created (201/200), but the record does not exist in the database.
- Phantom 404s: Downstream users and systems attempting to resolve or fetch the link experience unexpected 404 errors with no traceable origin.
- Zero Visibility: Because the exception was caught and suppressed in the service layer, it never bubbled up to the global HttpExceptionFilter. As a result, no alert or error log was generated, blinding on-call engineers.
- DECIDE Tradeoff Linkage: In DECIDE, Option B (graceful degradation) was chosen. This failure demonstrates the fundamental risk of misguided graceful degradation: returning partial or fake success responses silently masks critical infrastructure failures.
